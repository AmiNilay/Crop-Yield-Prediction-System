How It Works

Three steps to your prediction

No registration, no data sharing. Just tell us about your farm.

01

Select your details

Choose your crop, state, season, and farm area. Weather data is fetched automatically from NASA POWER and Open-Meteo.

02

AI analyzes patterns

XGBoost processes 59 encoded features — weather, irrigation, fertilizer — across 50,000 historical district records.

03

Get yield + explanation

See your predicted yield in q/ha, plus a SHAP breakdown showing exactly which factors helped or hurt your crop.
